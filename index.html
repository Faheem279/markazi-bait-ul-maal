What the trust doesہمارا کام

A community institution serving orphans, widows and people in hardship, through the generosity of those who give.

Orphans and widows

Regular care for orphans and widows, including a monthly supply of essential food items for widows.

Disaster relief

Medical aid

Support for sick people and families facing a health emergency.

Marriage support

Help with the marriages of orphan girls and other deserving people.
